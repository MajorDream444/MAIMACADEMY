Depending on features used, the Academy may process: name and email; interest-list and consent status; identity verification and sign-in records; purchase amounts, Stripe checkout and refund references (not complete card numbers); account and lesson progress; optional reflections; service-credit and booking details; meeting participation and support correspondence; and limited server logs needed for security and delivery.

Purposes include receiving and confirming free reservations, providing the requested guide where enabled, managing consent and suppression, verifying identity, granting access after payment, enforcing cohort capacity, showing lessons and saved progress, scheduling included sessions, handling refunds/disputes, providing support, preventing abuse and meeting applicable financial/legal duties.

The current architecture uses or proposes Vercel for hosting, Supabase for identity and Academy records, Stripe for payment/fraud and tax processing, Resend for transactional communication, and Google Meet/Calendar for authorized live sessions. Airtable may receive a deliberate, limited operational projection if later implemented; a reservation-to-Airtable synchronization has not been confirmed as live. Providers may process information in multiple countries under their own security and data-processing arrangements. Any required transfer disclosures and legal basis must be reviewed before approval.

Group-session replays are an intended inclusion, but no participant's private disclosures should be recorded or published by default. Recording notices, participant choice, withdrawal practices and replay access restrictions must be established before the first session. Private 1:1 consultations are not recorded by default. Testimonials, public showcases and external research reuse require separate permission.

The proposed Academy member backend uses restricted access, per-user data controls and signed payment-event handling. Local tests do not establish that every hosted control is operating. Access should be limited to authorized personnel and required processors. Purchase/financial records must be retained for applicable legal purposes; account, reflection, support and recording retention periods, deletion schedules and backups remain **pending operational and legal approval**.

## 7. Cookies and usage information

Essential authentication and security cookies or similar technologies may be used when member access is enabled. Optional analytics, advertising tracking or unrelated audience profiling are not authorized by this draft notice. A final notice should identify any such services if implemented.

## 8. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping obligations, you may request access, correction, export, deletion or restriction of your personal information, object to processing where applicable, and unsubscribe from optional marketing. Contact [contact@majoraimindset.com](mailto:contact@majoraimindset.com). Verification may be needed before account-related changes. ## 9. Changes and effective notice
