## Keep public funnels separate

The founder explicitly deferred the existing public weekly Knowledge Sessions/webinar and public live-build demonstrations to their separate conversation/workstream. They support discovery and product signups. Do NOT add them as new inclusions, redesign their schedule or weave them into this build.

The paid 30-day implementation/community groups remain a distinct inclusion approved in the interview. They support practice, peer learning, being heard and sharing approaches. Their schedule/moderation must be defined before sale. Community members are not required to act as unpaid support staff.

## Preparation and service credits

Adopt the service-credit design for organization: one founding private-session credit, and three session credits for a separately purchased advisory package. Keep session credits, group eligibility and vault access distinct. Credits are internal service records; no wallet, tradable token or blockchain integration is required.

Before an advisory appointment, capture the problem, desired result, attempted approaches, relevant source material and decision needed. Agents can prepare a brief and possible approaches; Major reviews it before the session. Arrive prepared while remaining open to reframing the problem.

Credit issuance/redemption, double-booking protection, preparation review, appointment integration and 90-day/30-day enforcement are NOT implemented by the current code scaffold. Journey/vault authorization helpers are not a completed credit ledger. Package session length, redemption windows, cancellations, no-shows, refunds and provider-unavailability accommodations remain OPEN. Ordinary correction of missing/incorrect purchased material should be distinguished from additional consulting in final terms.

## Interview insights for curriculum

- First learner example: an experienced practitioner with useful recordings, workbooks and teaching material, but an unorganized archive and an unclear offer. Use an anonymized case unless the person authorizes being featured.
- Founder story: conversation capture can support reviewed follow-up briefs with decisions, open questions and next actions. Distinguish words spoken from actual agreement; do not claim a described automation is independently verified live.
- Founder story: one intake location, consistent naming/tagging and duplicate review can make materials findable. Begin with copies/sample files; deletion and private client data need separate controls.
- Teaching frame: coach digital workers with a role, clear instructions, examples, boundaries and review.
- Proposed early outcome: organize a small resource set, answer one recurring question and create one useful resource for one defined person.
- Implementation matters beyond purchase. Identify barriers, a small next action and observable benefit; no automatic conversion or financial outcome promise.
- Reuse a core resource, then allow bounded individual refinement. The founder's one-to-four-hour estimate combines initial building and client work; measure these separately during the pilot.
- Capture practitioner-specific terminology with plain-language meanings and practical examples; check the client's understanding.
- Offer read/hear/see/do formats where useful. Generated discussion audio requires review and labeling; no such audio has been produced here.
