# Founding journey — owner decisions, 19 October 2026

Confirmed in founder interview:
- One-time purchase, not an Academy subscription.
- Founding learners keep this journey and its future revisions; no automatic entitlement to other courses, vault passes, coaching or future products.
- Learning outcome: solve one real problem and finish with a small useful result.
- Price: decide after scope; no price/product was created in Stripe.
- Optional future vault access: 1, 3, 6 or 12 calendar months. No automatic renewal is implemented. Pricing, included assets and delivery/support still require definition.

Do not advertise an unconditional forever service guarantee. Publish clear access/hosting, refund and support terms before selling. Founding access has no routine expiry in the application model; refund, fraud/dispute holds and lawful account restrictions remain separate.

The vault must earn its value: owner-curated research, advanced practical resources, reusable playbooks or other rights-cleared work. Examples are proposals, not a stocked/live vault. Keep the journey operational independently of a vault pass.
