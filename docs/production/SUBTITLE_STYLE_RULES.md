# Approved subtitle and active-word highlight rules

Approved by Major Dream Williams on 26 October 2026. Applies to all production agents, editors, and caption renderers.

“Prompter” means the active spoken word or short phrase highlighted in sync with speech; it does not mean a teleprompter.

| Brand/channel | Base subtitle | Active-word/phrase highlight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MAIM Academy | White text, black outline | Gold |
| Major AI Mindset | White text, black outline | Blue or orange |

Use canonical brand color tokens from the production design system. Keep white as the dominant caption color. Choose blue or orange deliberately for Major AI Mindset and maintain consistency within the asset.

Burn stylized subtitles into the post-production video and also supply a corrected SRT or VTT for optional YouTube closed captions. Keep captions readable on phones, at most two lines, with restrained animation. Avoid covering the presenter's face, Academy crest, CTA, or teaching graphics. Highlight the active spoken word or short phrase in sync; never imply precise word timing has been verified unless checked against audio.

Correct MAIM, MAIM Academy, Major AI Mindset, YAWDS/YARDIE, and URLs in captions. Preserve the speaker's meaning; factual corrections must not silently rewrite what was spoken.

These are standing production rules, not a claim that existing videos have been rendered or updated.
