# 30-Day Major Moves Journey — proposal

Status: proposed activities, not an approved school curriculum or independently validated learning outcome. Scope: a browsable static sample at /major-moves, no stored progress or learner portfolio backend.

## Provenance

The founder supplied an editorial assessment of NotebookLM's Interactive 30-Day Onboarding Journey and Major AI Manifesto in the 19 October 2026 conversation. That assessment is the source for the broad structure and safeguards. The original unabridged NotebookLM output was not available in this checkout and is NOT reconstructed or claimed preserved here. The daily activities in data/major-moves.json are newly authored proposals, not quotations from NotebookLM or the books. Attached PDFs were not read in this implementation. Chapter-level book/source mapping remains OPEN on every activity.

ABC definition is established by Major-AI-OS README: Awareness, Belief, Context, Direction, Experiment. Earlier uncertainty in chat is resolved by this repository source. These are iterative tools, not mandatory ideological commitments.

## Sequence and schema

Days 1–7: Awareness and Belief. Days 8–14: Context and Skills. Days 15–21: Direction and Protection. Days 22–28: Spark Lab experiment and legacy. Days 29–30: reflection and next move. Day 26 demonstrates a revised prototype rather than starting an overlapping five-day challenge. Pillar 7 supports sustainable practice throughout.

Machine-readable lesson fields: day, title, stage, pillars, learn, explore, major_move, artifact, reflection, completion, source_status, age_adaptation. JSON is the single activity source for the route. Completion means creating an artifact and explaining a choice; it does not mean externally accepted competence. The current page displays draft learning activities, not finished instructional media. Lesson learn prompts need facilitator-approved examples before teaching a cohort.

Learners may use a private notebook or local files. This release saves no progress and claims no account, upload, dashboard or operational handoff. Later progress records should be learner-controlled and separately reviewed for privacy, access, export and deletion.

## Safeguards and pilot gates

No mandatory fasting, wallet, transaction, public manifesto, private contact map or cultural training dataset. Use fictional data, permission-cleared examples and supervised youth collaboration. Manifesto endorsement is optional; learners can question or write their own commitments. Do not publish minor identities or collect passwords. Legal lessons require jurisdiction-specific verified sources before addition; none is taught here as settled law.

Before a learner pilot: attach approved original passages and permissions; develop complete age-specific lessons; facilitator review; accessibility/offline review; practical comprehension check; time-on-task estimates tested with learners; consent/retention policy if any data collection is introduced. A static activity catalog is not a claim that these gates passed.

## Evaluation proposal

Use a small facilitated pilot. Review whether learners can explain a choice, check a claim, improve a prototype and choose a next step. Record optional aggregate feedback under an approved protocol; avoid mandatory streaks or public leaderboards. Spark Lab and Foundry participation require their own separate scope and authorization.
