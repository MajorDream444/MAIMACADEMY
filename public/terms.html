Founding Cohort Terms of Service

Proposed customer terms for the 30-Day Major Moves Journey and the November 2026 founding cohort.

1. Provider and program

MAIM Academy is an educational program associated with Major AI Mindset. Contracting legal entity, registered address and governing law remain subject to founder and legal confirmation. These terms are proposed for adult participants who purchase the founding cohort. The Academy is separate from AMA Solutions' consulting products and from the MAIM Client System Blueprint.

2. Price, capacity and what is included

The founding journey costs USD $249, paid once, plus applicable taxes disclosed at checkout. It is intended for experienced practitioners and business owners and initially accepts a maximum of 20 paid learners. Free interest-list reservations do not guarantee a paid seat.

The founding offer includes the 30-Day Major Moves learning journey; four 60-minute live group implementation sessions; access to reviewed group-session replays; and one private 45-minute session with Major Dream Williams to be used within 90 days after enrollment, subject to the scheduling rules below. The journey and future content revisions remain accessible while the service is operated, without a routine course-access expiry. This is not an unconditional lifetime-hosting promise.

Unrelated courses, bespoke development, paid advisory packages, software subscriptions, separate Knowledge Vault passes, and broader MAIM products are not included unless expressly stated.

3. Cohort dates, delivery and changes

The first live group session is Wednesday 18 November 2026 at 14:00 UTC (22:00 Bali). The planned continuation sessions are 25 November, 2 December and 9 December 2026 at the same time, pending the host's final confirmation. Enrollment instructions will provide verified joining information privately. We will make reasonable efforts to communicate significant schedule changes promptly and provide a reasonable alternative if delivery is materially disrupted.

Session replays are included with the journey, with a target of publication within 48 hours after recording and review. Recording or publication is subject to participant privacy, content suitability and necessary consent; replays may be clean teaching recaps rather than recordings of private disclosures. Learners may submit questions if they cannot attend.

4. Private session, booking and preparation

One included 45-minute session must be used within 90 days of enrollment, with extensions when provider availability or cancellation prevents timely use. Participants provide a short preparation brief at least 48 hours before the appointment for review. Appointments are scheduled after the initial seven-day refund-request period. Reschedule with at least 24 hours' notice when possible; one courtesy late reschedule is allowed, and exceptions receive human review. Actual booking availability and private Google Meet details must be provided in the authenticated member experience or a verified scheduling channel.

5. Seven-day refund request policy

A participant may request a refund within seven days of purchase by contacting contact@majoraimindset.com; no proof of completed coursework or effort is required. Requests after that window are assessed under applicable consumer law and any separately approved circumstances. Statutory rights are not limited. A full refund generally ends the corresponding paid access and unused service credits, subject to applicable law and correction of administrative errors. The exact implementation, country-specific cancellation rights, and legal wording require final review.

6. Learning expectations and acceptable use

The program teaches applied systems thinking and practical AI workflows. Participants remain responsible for evaluating tools, using only materials they have rights to use, and protecting their own and other people's confidential information. Do not upload passwords, full card numbers, private third-party records or materials you are not authorized to share. Outputs and examples are educational; no revenue, client acquisition, qualification, business outcome or automatic Foundry acceptance is guaranteed.

7. Intellectual property and learner submissions

Learners retain their pre-existing work and intellectual property. MAIM Academy retains its pre-existing instruction, templates, lesson materials, branding and frameworks. A purchase grants a personal, non-transferable right to access the purchased learning materials for individual learning and internal business application while access is provided. It does not grant resale, redistribution or public republication of Academy content. Learner reflections and business examples will not be used publicly as testimonials or case studies without separate permission.

8. Accounts, privacy and recordings

Member access will require a verified identity. Payment processing is expected through Stripe; the platform also uses or proposes using Supabase, Vercel, Resend and scheduling providers for restricted delivery. Consult the Academy Privacy Notice candidate for details. Cohort call recording and participation disclosures must be finalized and communicated before sessions. Recording private coaching or participant disclosures is not authorized by default.

9. Support, service interruptions and changes

For receipt issues, missing access, cancellation, schedule or accommodation questions, contact contact@majoraimindset.com. Reasonable access and delivery issues will be investigated. Material changes to an accepted purchase's terms require appropriate notice and, where necessary, consent. Any liability limits, dispute resolution provisions, business address, jurisdiction and consumer disclosures are pending legal approval; none are invented here.

10. Approval record

This is a proposed agreement only. Before collecting checkout consent, approve the provider's legal name/address, governing law, privacy notice, schedule, recording notices, support policy, effective date and an immutable final terms version. Do not silently substitute these terms for the separate MAIM Blueprint service terms.
