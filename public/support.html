What to include in a request

Tell us whether your question concerns a free reservation, a payment receipt, learning-room access, a session or a refund. Include the checkout email address and, if you have one, the Stripe receipt reference. Never email payment-card numbers, account credentials or unrelated private client documents.

Charges and refunds

The founding cohort's advertised price is USD $249 once. Payment invitations are gated by release approval; free reservations are not purchases. For a refund request, email support within seven days of purchase as set out in the proposed founding-cohort terms. Applicable consumer rights remain in force.

Access and booking

For missing lessons, account problems, calendars, 45-minute coaching or cohort replay questions, contact support. Bookings and provider availability are not guaranteed by a free interest-list reservation.
